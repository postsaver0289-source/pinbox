Privacy Policy for Pinbox

Last updated: 2025

Pinbox ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Pinbox browser extension and website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our extension.

Information We Collect

Pinbox does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. All actions (sharing links, copying URLs) happen locally in your browser and are sent directly to the third-party platform you choose (e.g., Twitter, Facebook, WhatsApp).

Permissions

Pinbox requests browser permissions only to display a right-click menu and a floating share button on supported websites. These permissions are used solely to provide the sharing functionality and nothing else.

Third-Party Services

When you use Pinbox to share content, you are redirected to third-party services (Twitter, Facebook, WhatsApp). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
